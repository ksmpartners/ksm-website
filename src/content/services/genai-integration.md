## Generative AI Integration Services

Generative AI has generated enormous excitement — and an equal measure of hype, fear, uncertainty, and doubt. Like the open-source software wave before it, the technology is real and the productivity gains are genuine. But for organizations in regulated industries, realizing those gains responsibly requires more than plugging in an API key.

### Regulated Industry Focus

KSM Technology Partners helps clients in regulated industries harness generative AI across their business and software development operations. We bring the controls, governance, and technical rigor that let you move fast without exposing your organization to data, compliance, or reputational risk.

### Capabilities

**Risk Assessment and Control Identification**
We analyze your generative AI needs and deliver a structured assessment of your organization's AI risk posture: what data will the system touch, how sensitive is it, what are the failure modes if the model is wrong, and what is the regulatory surface area? We then identify the appropriate controls to mitigate these risks and ensure compliance.

**Hosting Selection and Implementation**
We help you evaluate the full landscape: self-hosted open-weight models via Ollama for air-gapped or on-premises requirements; managed private deployments via AWS Bedrock or Azure OpenAI Service for teams that need cloud scale without public data exposure; or frontier models such as Anthropic Claude where capability requirements justify it and data handling agreements are in place.

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**
RAG allows existing AI models to answer questions grounded in your proprietary documents, operational data, and institutional knowledge base — without that data ever being used to train a shared model or exposed to third-party infrastructure. We build intelligent agents that can answer questions from diverse sources, and "show their work" by citing sources for every answer.

**Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server Development**
MCP — the emerging open standard for connecting AI models to enterprise data sources and tools — is rapidly becoming the integration layer of the agentic AI stack. We build MCP servers that let compliant AI clients securely call into your existing systems in a controlled, auditable way.

### Outcomes

- AI-assisted regulatory document drafting integrated into existing validation workflows — faster drafts, no additional compliance overhead
- On-premises model deployment delivering frontier-class capability with zero data leaving the client environment
- Generative AI risk-scoring framework adopted across multiple business units, with audit-ready documentation produced automatically
- RAG-powered knowledge assistant giving analysts faster, better-grounded answers on complex regulatory and market questions
