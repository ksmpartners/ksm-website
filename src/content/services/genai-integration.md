## Generative AI Integration Services

Generative AI has generated enormous excitement — and an equal measure of hype, fear, uncertainty, and doubt. Like the open-source software wave before it, the technology is real and the productivity gains are genuine. But for organizations in regulated industries, realizing those gains responsibly requires more than plugging in an API key.

### Regulated Industry Focus

KSM Technology Partners helps clients in regulated industries harness generative AI across their business and software development operations. We bring the controls, governance, and technical rigor that let you move fast without exposing your organization to data, compliance, or reputational risk.

**Life Sciences**

Clinical data is among the most sensitive in any enterprise. Patient privacy obligations, regulatory data integrity expectations, and the fundamental scientific requirement to *show your work* all constrain how and where AI can be applied. AI-assisted outputs that contribute to regulatory submissions must carry a defensible chain of custody back to the source. And like any software enlisted in GxP processes, generative AI tooling must be subjected to a risk-based computer software assurance regimen — not assumed to be correct because it came from a reputable vendor.

**Utilities**

Electrical power market operations and grid reliability functions handle categories of operational and infrastructure data that carry strict confidentiality obligations under federal cybersecurity standards — data that cannot be shared with a third-party system, including a public AI service, without specific protective controls documented and in place. Beyond data handling, the operational stakes are high: an AI-assisted forecast or dispatch recommendation that is wrong at the wrong moment is not just an embarrassment — it can affect grid reliability. Latency, availability, and fail-safe behavior must be designed in from the start.

### Capabilities

**Risk Mitigation**
We begin every engagement with a structured assessment of your organization's AI risk posture: what data will the system touch, how sensitive is it, what are the failure modes if the model is wrong, and what is the regulatory surface area? We then identify the appropriate controls to mitigate these risks and ensure compliance.

**Governance**
Identifying controls is only half the work. The other half is ensuring those controls remain in place over time and can be demonstrated to auditors on demand. We design governance frameworks that specify which models are approved for which use cases, how prompts and outputs are logged, how model versions are tracked, and how policy exceptions are reviewed. 

**Model and Hosting Selection**
We help you evaluate the full landscape: self-hosted open-weight models via Ollama for air-gapped or on-premises requirements; managed private deployments via AWS Bedrock or Azure OpenAI Service for teams that need cloud scale without public data exposure; or frontier models such as Anthropic Claude where capability requirements justify it and data handling agreements are in place.

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**
RAG allows an AI model to answer questions grounded in your proprietary documents, operational data, and institutional knowledge — without that data ever being used to train a shared model or exposed to third-party infrastructure. 

**Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server Development**
MCP — the emerging open standard for connecting AI models to enterprise data sources and tools — is rapidly becoming the integration layer of the agentic AI stack. An MCP-based architecture lets compliant AI clients securely call into your existing systems in a standardized, auditable way.

### Outcomes

- AI-assisted regulatory document drafting integrated into existing validation workflows — faster drafts, no additional compliance overhead
- On-premises model deployment delivering frontier-class capability with zero data leaving the client environment
- Generative AI governance framework adopted across multiple business units, with audit-ready documentation produced automatically
- RAG-powered knowledge assistant giving analysts faster, better-grounded answers on complex regulatory and market questions
